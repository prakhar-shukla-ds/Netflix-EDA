## Project Overview

This project presents an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of the Netflix dataset using Python. The objective is to explore Netflix's content library, identify trends and patterns, and generate actionable business insights through data cleaning, visualization, and analysis.

The project follows a complete EDA workflow, including data understanding, preprocessing, exploratory analysis, visualization, and business recommendations.

---

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to answer the following business questions:

- What type of content dominates the Netflix catalogue?
- Which content ratings are most common?
- How has Netflix's content library evolved over the years?
- Which countries contribute the most content?
- What are the most common genres?
- What business insights can be derived from the data?

## Key Insights

- Movies account for approximately **70%** of the Netflix catalogue.
- **TV-MA** and **TV-14** are the most common content ratings, indicating a strong focus on teenage and adult audiences.
- Most content available on Netflix has been released in recent years.
- The **United States**, **India**, and the **United Kingdom** contribute the highest number of titles.
- Movie durations are concentrated around standard feature-length films.
- Netflix offers content across a wide variety of genres to serve diverse audience preferences.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Increase investment in TV Shows to improve long-term viewer engagement.
- Expand family-friendly content to attract a broader audience.
- Continue investing in recently released content.
- Strengthen regional content strategies while expanding localized content in emerging markets.
- Maintain a balanced mix of Movies and TV Shows.
